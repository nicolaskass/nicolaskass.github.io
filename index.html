How I work

What I want my students to leave with

Wherever I teach, I work the same way: identify the problem, design a solution, solve it, then assess and adjust. What I try to leave in my students is their own criteria: how to approach a problem and where to look when they don't know the answer. I'm proudest when an exam shows me a student reasoning their way through a problem.

I use AI as a tool, and the judgement stays with me

I built my own workflow on Claude Code, with separate agents for coding, reviewing and documenting. I do the planning: the AI writes most of the code, and I make the decisions, set the criteria and audit the result. We all have the same tools; what changes the result is the person guiding them.

I write down what a decision cost

Every decision record has a section for its downsides. A document that lists only benefits is marketing, and it makes everything around it less believable.

I know where automation should stop

In the surveying tools the software flags anomalies and corrects none of them. A field measurement is evidence, and the person qualified to judge it is the one who took it.
